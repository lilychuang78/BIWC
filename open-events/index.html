Open Events

We hold two open events every month: the Monthly Meeting and the Meet & Greet. This is the best way to meet new people, and to see if the BIWC is a good fit. These events are open to all women, there is no need to register, just show up! We watch out for new faces, and really look forward to meeting you.

Take a look at the ACTIVITIES page for a complete view of all activities we offer our members.

Monthly Meeting

Join us the first Tuesday evening of every month at a restaurant for a no-host dinner. Sometimes a speaker presents a topic of interest; sometimes we play a trivia game just for fun. We always enjoy seeing new faces, whether you’ve been in Berlin for ages, are brand new to the city. This is a wonderful way to get to know our club, our community of women.

When: Tuesday, August 4, 6pm

Where: Restaurant Monte Mente in The Center (previously Sony Center), Potsdamer Platz, Bellevuestraße 5, 10785 Berlin

Meet and Greet

Pop in and join us for coffee at Kaffeehaus Mila at 10:30am on the 22nd of every month.

Bring your baby or your knitting if you want, it’s all about connecting. We chat over a cappuccino or a mint tea, maybe a chocolate croissant or a feta salad. For sure, we just appreciate coming together, pausing before attacking the must-do’s of the day. Many of our new members first experience our club through a Meet and Greet.

When: The 22nd of every month, 10:30am

Where: Kaffeehaus Mila, Grolmanstraße 40, 10623 Berlin SBahn Savignyplatz, UBahn Uhlandstraße
